Setup: load your own lists

Runs only on this computer; nothing you paste leaves this page. Use it for procedures and lists, never for client data. Then click "Download customized copy" and use that file from now on.

2 columns = Disposition, Definition. 3 or more = Group, Disposition, Definition. A header row is skipped automatically.
Two columns (Rule, What to do), or "Rule | What to do" per line. They appear as a checklist next to the note.

Name, phone and email go only in the CRM-only fields (for copying into the CRM) and never into the note. The note will not copy while a phone, email, address, ID, date of birth, plate, or claim/policy/record number is detected.

In free-text fields, describe roles (the other driver, the store manager), not names or numbers.

Do not paste this note, or any part of it, into an AI tool. The form clears after 10 minutes of inactivity and when the tab closes.
