Intake Note Builder

Generic template. Builds the case note for your CRM. Runs offline; nothing is saved.

Name, email and phone go straight into the CRM, not this form. The note will not copy while a phone, email, address, ID, date of birth, plate, or claim/policy/record number is detected.

In free-text fields, describe roles (the other driver, the store manager), not names or numbers.

Do not paste this note, or any part of it, into an AI tool. The form clears after 10 minutes of inactivity and when the tab closes.
